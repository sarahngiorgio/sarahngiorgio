## Equilo

**Equilo** is designed to help people move through emotional moments with greater awareness, steadiness, and choice.

The product brings together:

- **Immediate Support** for low-friction access to practical exercises
- **Check-Ins** that help users identify what they are experiencing
- **Skills** drawn from evidence-informed psychological frameworks
- **Reflection** that supports meaning-making without requiring overanalysis
- **Pattern Awareness** that helps users recognize recurring experiences over time

Equilo is positioned between meditation apps, mood trackers, journaling tools, educational skill libraries, and therapy platforms. Its focus is structured emotional self-support: more guided than open-ended journaling, more actionable than passive mood tracking, and intentionally non-clinical.

## Product Principles

Equilo is built around several core principles:

- Immediate support should be easy to reach.
- Reflection should reduce cognitive friction, not create more of it.
- Recommendations should be governed, explainable, and bounded.
- Users should remain the authors of their own meaning.
- Emotional experiences should not be gamified, judged, or treated as failures.
- Accessibility, privacy, safety, and cognitive load are product requirements.
- The goal is not to eliminate emotion, but to support more intentional responses.

The experience is designed to feel steady, intelligent, grounded, private, calm, and credible.

## Product and Engineering Approach

Equilo is being developed as a modular product system with clear boundaries between:

- governed educational content
- transient user interactions
- deterministic recommendation logic
- immediate-support experiences
- reflection and pattern-awareness capabilities
- safety, privacy, accessibility, and claims controls

Current development emphasizes:

- mobile-first and accessible interaction design
- deterministic, explainable behavior
- explicit failure states and neutral fallbacks
- controlled handling of sensitive emotional data
- TypeScript-first full-stack architecture
- automated validation and regression testing
- GitHub-based issues, pull requests, and decision records
- GitHub Actions as the authoritative continuous-integration gate

---

## Safety and Scope

Equilo is an educational support tool. It is not therapy, medical care, crisis care, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for a qualified professional or emergency service.

This boundary is part of the product architecture. Immediate Support remains independently accessible, sensitive functionality is designed to fail closed to a neutral state, and product language avoids unsupported clinical or safety claims.

## Current Direction

Equilo is under active development.

Current work centers on an integrated guest experience with structured check-ins, governed recommendations, transient results, browser lifecycle safeguards, and independent Immediate Support. Durable personal data, accounts, stored patterns, analytics, and subscriptions remain separate future capabilities that require their own privacy, ownership, and implementation decisions.
